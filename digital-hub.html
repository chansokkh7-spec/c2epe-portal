🔒 Privacy & Data Notice

🔒 Privacy & Data Notice

What we store: your child's Student ID and name (given out by their teacher), which lessons/systems they open, and their quiz scores/answers. We do not collect passwords, addresses, or payment details for students.

Who can see it: school admins, and anyone who has the Student ID -- a parent can view the same "My Progress" report using the same ID the teacher gave the student. There is no separate public listing.

How it's used: only to show progress to the student, their parent, and their teacher/admin. It is never sold or shared with advertisers or other third parties.

Security note: a Student ID works like a shared class code rather than a personal password -- treat it the way you'd treat a library card number, and ask your teacher for a new one if you think someone else has it.

Requests: to review, correct, or delete a student's data, contact the school (SEG Education Group / C2-EPE Digital Academy) directly.
